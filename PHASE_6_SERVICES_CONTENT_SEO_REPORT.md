## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 6 of the Master SEO Implementation Project establishes a high-converting, organically dominant **Service Landing Page & Travel Content SEO Architecture** for Rider Call Taxi. 

Prior to this phase, the website had a single generic `/services` page, limiting Google's ability to rank the website for high-intent, transactional queries such as *"One Way Taxi in Sankarankovil"*, *"Airport Taxi to Madurai"*, or *"Outstation Cab Sankarankovil"*. Furthermore, there was no informational content to capture top-of-funnel regional road travelers and tourists planning trips to Tenkasi, Courtallam, Tirunelveli, or Madurai Airport.

### Key Milestones Achieved in Phase 6:
1. **8 Dedicated Service Landing Pages:** Fully implemented with custom URLs, single `<h1>` hierarchy, verified fleet specifications, transparent slab tariffs, 3-step booking workflows, bilingual English/Tamil toggling, and dedicated `Service` & `ItemPage` JSON-LD schemas.
2. **Travel Content & Blog Hub (`/blog`):** Authoring and deployment of 6 comprehensive, original travel articles covering genuine highway distances, verified slab fare comparisons, local route guidance, airport buffer guidelines, and outstation safety advice.
3. **Structured Data Ecosystem:** Extended dynamic `#route-dynamic-jsonld` integration to inject `BlogPosting`, `Blog`, `Service`, and `BreadcrumbList` schemas across all 15 newly created routes.
4. **Site Architecture & Internal Linking:** Updated desktop navbar, mobile drawer navigation, and footer columns with direct links to service pages and travel guides.
5. **XML Sitemap Expansion:** Expanded `public/sitemap.xml` to 24 indexable, canonical HTTPS URLs.
